# Contributing to Multa Lumina

**Curiosity is welcome. Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.**

## Where can I help?

- Suggest a realistic problem from a workplace or an unfamiliar home.
- Pose a question that can be tested.
- Reproduce a reported result or document a useful failure.
- Build or improve agent capabilities and test environments.
- Help with plain-English explanations, translation, accessibility, or research review.

AI-assisted and vibe-coded contributions are welcome. Please test your submissions and explain the assistance you used rather than representing generated work as independently verified.

## Your first contribution

1. Read [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md) and the [two official challenges](README.md#the-only-two-finish-lines).
2. Check the [research dashboard](https://github.com/users/rajivamin/projects/1) and [existing issues](https://github.com/rajivamin/multa-lumina/issues) for similar work; join [Discussions](https://github.com/rajivamin/multa-lumina/discussions).
3. Propose a question stating **problem, relevance, proposed test, expected observation, and contributor role**.
4. Report experiments using [the research record template](research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) and [discovery process](docs/DISCOVERY_PROCESS.md).
5. Link earlier research and acknowledge all collaborators.
6. If claiming a discovery, include independently checkable evidence and limitations.

## Research status vocabulary

**Proposed:** Idea only. **Exploring:** Work in progress. **Reported:** Outcome claimed, review pending. **Reproduced:** Another evaluator repeated the specific result. **Validated:** Evidence independently accepted for the specific claim, not necessarily either complete test. **Disputed / Superseded:** Challenged or corrected result.

A Full Job or Coffee Test pass requires a preregistered, independent, end-to-end evaluation. No isolated benchmark counts as a pass.

## Safety, privacy and permissions

Never include company secrets, real customer records, tokens, personal household recordings, or other materials without explicit permission. Do not run agents in an employer's systems or someone's home without authorization and risk controls.

The repository currently uses [Apache 2.0](LICENSE). Read the draft [contributor rights and discovery attribution policy](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) before contributing. Avoid patent-sensitive disclosures until you have obtained appropriate advice and the final IP procedure is established.

**Low barrier to participation. High bar for discovery claims.**
