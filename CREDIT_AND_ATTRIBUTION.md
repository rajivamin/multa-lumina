# Contributor Rights & Discovery Attribution

**Founding policy draft.** This is a public framework for fair credit, not a signed contributor agreement or a guarantee of legal priority. Formal IP and patent-sensitive procedures require review.

## Principles

1. **Evidence over credentials.** Beginners, professional researchers, and people using AI coding assistants can all make genuine contributions.
2. **Credit is specific.** An original insight, an implementation, an independent replication, and decisive improvement are different accomplishments. Record each.
3. **Trace dependencies.** Each significant claim must link the earlier work on which it relies.
4. **Permanent, correctable records.** Preserve publicly auditable histories, with justified privacy exceptions. Make corrections append-only when possible.
5. **No automatic authorship.** Participating does not automatically qualify someone as an author of a resulting paper; authorship decisions must be justified transparently.
6. **Time is evidence, not proof.** A timestamp demonstrates that materials existed by a date, not necessarily that they were original or that the submitter owns an invention.

## Discovery ledger (proposed)

Each accepted finding should include a stable ID, version, original submission link, contributor identities or handles and roles, a precise claim, methods, evidence, limitations, references to antecedents, independent reviewer decisions, and correction links. Preserve content hashes and independent archives for significant public results when feasible.

Use established [CRediT roles](https://credit.niso.org/) where applicable alongside plain-language descriptions. AI systems and tools must be disclosed as contributors to methodology or assistance where material, but human responsibility remains identifiable.

## Challenges and disputes

Report suspected misattribution privately to maintainers rather than publishing allegations. An uninvolved reviewer should consider the evidence, give affected contributors a chance to respond, publish a justified correction when appropriate, and enable independent appeal where practical. The [private reporting protocol](docs/PRIVATE_REPORTING.md) is published, but the dedicated intake contact must still be activated before wide public recruitment.

## Copyright, licensing and patents

The repository contains the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Accepted contributions are subject to the applicable license, including its contributor and patent provisions. Attribution under this document does **not** override license permissions or automatically assign ownership of scientific discoveries.

Contributors should not submit unpublished patent-sensitive inventions, confidential data, or materials they lack authority to license without obtaining appropriate advice. Contribution and research-content terms will be reviewed before substantial code or sensitive research is solicited.

**Goal:** Protect the evidence trail for genuine contributions, while recognizing that no record can stop someone else from making a false external claim.
