## How a record is added

1. Submit a research record using [research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md](research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) and the [discovery submission process](docs/DISCOVERY_PROCESS.md).
2. Preserve links to prior work, all named human contributors, AI assistance, methods, limits and test outputs.
3. A reviewer checks the *record completeness*, the evidence and any claimed dependencies; independent replication is separately documented when claimed.
4. Assign a unique immutable-style identifier such as `ML-R-000001` and link the accepted record and its original pull request or discussion.
5. Later corrections appear as additional linked notes with dates and rationale. Do not silently delete the original record.

**Important:** A GitHub timestamp or cryptographic digest is evidence of the presence of particular material at a given time, not proof of originality, inventorship, or legal title. A finding may be reported, reproduced, or validated **only for the specific claim and evidence examined**.

For attribution questions see [CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md). Please do not make public accusations about a named person.
