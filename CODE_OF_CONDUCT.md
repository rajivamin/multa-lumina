# Multa Lumina Community Conduct

This initiative welcomes participants across disciplines, experience levels, ages, backgrounds, and AI-tool preferences.

- Critique evidence and methods rather than identities, credentials, or skill levels.
- Cite original contributions and disclose significant AI assistance.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, publish private information, or knowingly falsify results.
- Never share confidential workplace materials, customer information, or private household data without permission.
- Treat attribution concerns seriously, and allow fair evidence-based review and correction.
- Preserve a respectful environment for beginners and experts alike.

The [private reporting and review procedure](docs/PRIVATE_REPORTING.md) is published, but its confidential intake mailbox has not yet been activated. Do not disclose sensitive allegations publicly in issues or Discussions.
