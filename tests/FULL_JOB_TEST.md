# The Full Job Test

**Status: Proposed evaluation protocol, not a claimed result.**

## Objective

Determine whether an AI system can autonomously fulfill the normal workload of **one** economically valuable, full-time remote human role at an unfamiliar organization over an extended period. Example roles include remote software engineer, financial analyst, or customer success representative. The candidate need not pass all three.

## Essential conditions

1. **Unfamiliar onboarding:** The system joins an employer without specialized company-specific retraining, a hidden operator playbook, or memorized private company materials. It may read ordinary documentation and receive ordinary assignments.
2. **Whole job, not isolated tasks:** It must handle routine work, ambiguity, meetings and collaboration when necessary, changing policies, unexpected assignments, and appropriate escalation.
3. **Sustained performance:** A trial spanning weeks or months is required. A 90-day period is an initial proposal, not yet an adopted rule.
4. **No covert operator:** Coworkers can interact with the AI as with an employee, but must not secretly produce its deliverables, choose each next step, or troubleshoot its private decision-making.
5. **Economic value:** Independent evaluators assess workload, professional quality, reliability, timeliness, remediation costs, and full operating expenses (including inference and supervision).
6. **Independent auditability:** Preserve privacy-respecting evidence of assignments, tools, configuration, interventions, mistakes, and work outcomes.
7. **Consent and safety:** No unauthorized access to employers, production systems, accounts, customer records, or financial transactions.

## Set these terms before any official trial

Specify the job and workload; host organization's consent; start/end dates; acceptable quality and economic thresholds; allowed coworker interactions; prohibited assistance; candidate configuration; auditing, privacy and safety rules; independent evaluators; and invalidation conditions.

## Outcomes

- **Passed:** Independently verified complete fulfillment of the preregistered job under every essential condition.
- **Not passed:** One or more essential conditions was not met.
- **Inconclusive:** Evidence is insufficient, or the evaluation was invalidated or interrupted.

A good coding benchmark score or an afternoon of customer support is **not** a Full Job Test pass. See the [research record template](../research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).
