# The Wozniak Coffee Test

**Status: Proposed evaluation protocol, not a claimed result.**

## Objective

Determine whether a physical AI system can safely prepare a cup of coffee after entering a genuinely unfamiliar American home, without assistance or environment-specific preparation.

## Essential conditions

1. **Unfamiliar home:** Independent evaluators select a home with homeowner consent. The robot has no rehearsed layout or preprogrammed map for it.
2. **Autonomous discovery:** The system finds the kitchen, equipment, coffee supplies, water, and other needed items without guidance.
3. **Equipment understanding:** It figures out how to use the coffee-making setup as actually encountered, within a defined safety envelope.
4. **Complete outcome:** It prepares coffee and leaves the area in a safe condition.
5. **No hidden assistance:** No teleoperation, off-camera guidance, rehearsed placement of ingredients, or human prompting during the run.
6. **Safety and privacy:** Human safety monitors may stop unsafe actions; a stop requiring task intervention cannot count as an unassisted pass. Record only what homeowners consent to.
7. **Independent verification:** Preserve a privacy-conscious record, intervention log, system configuration, and independent replication.

## Preregister before the official test

Define unfamiliarity, home selection, allowed robot capabilities, coffee preparation criteria, safety limits, handling of appliances and hot liquids, recording/retention consent, reviewers, replication requirements, and invalidation rules.

## Outcomes

- **Passed:** Complete autonomous success under the preregistered conditions, independently verified and replicated.
- **Not passed:** One or more essential conditions was not met.
- **Inconclusive:** The evaluation was interrupted, unsafe, invalid, or insufficiently documented.

Brewing coffee in a prepared exhibition kitchen does **not** establish a pass of this challenge. See the [research record template](../research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).
