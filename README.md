# MULTA LUMINA

**Many lights. One pursuit.**

*An open, collaborative AI autonomy research initiative.*

## Our mission

Can humans and independently developed AI systems collaborate to build or assemble a system that can **completely** perform an economically valuable full-time human job, or autonomously make coffee in an unfamiliar home?

Multa Lumina is an invitation to find out. No minimum number of developers, required model provider, deadline, or promised breakthrough. Every experience level is welcome, including beginners, vibe coders, domain experts, and professional researchers.

## The only two finish lines

### 1. The Full Job Test

An AI system joins an unfamiliar company as a remote software engineer, financial analyst, customer success representative, or another agreed economically valuable full-time role. It independently handles onboarding, documentation, assignments, collaboration, unexpected situations, and changing policies across **weeks or months**, without specialized employer-specific retraining or secret human operators. Ordinary interactions with colleagues are allowed. Economic value and independent evaluation are essential.

[Full Job Test: proposed evaluation protocol](tests/FULL_JOB_TEST.md).

### 2. The Wozniak Coffee Test

A robot or embodied AI enters an unfamiliar American home, finds the kitchen, discovers the available equipment and ingredients, and prepares a cup of coffee without special preparation or human assistance. Trials must be consented, safe, independently reviewed, and replicated.

[Wozniak Coffee Test: proposed evaluation protocol](tests/COFFEE_TEST.md).

**Passing either test accomplishes the central objective.** An isolated successful task, exciting demo, or benchmark improvement is not a pass of the full challenge.

## Start participating

**New to research or coding?** Begin with [START HERE](START_HERE.md). You can propose realistic workplace or household scenarios without writing any code.

**AI-assisted builder or professional developer?** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), browse Issues, and use our [experiment reporting template](research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).

**Domain expert?** Help explain what real competence looks like and design scenarios that challenge shallow demonstrations.

## How work is organized

- **Issues:** Experiment proposals, actionable investigations, replication requests, and documentation tasks.
- **Discussions:** Community questions and knowledge sharing, once enabled.
- **Research records:** Reproducible methods, evidence, negative findings, and limitations.
- **Attribution ledger:** Linked records of original ideas, implementations, independent verification, dependencies, and corrections. This process is being established in the [founding credit policy](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).

We distinguish *proposed*, *reported*, *reproduced*, and *validated* findings. Only an independent preregistered end-to-end evaluation can certify a completed challenge.

## Responsible research and contributor rights

- All actual workplace and home testing requires permission, appropriate safeguards, and privacy controls.
- Do not submit secrets, customer data, private recordings, unauthorized access instructions, or fabricated evidence.
- AI-assisted coding is welcome when accurately disclosed and tested.
- Contributors receive credit based on evidence and their actual work, not credentials.
- The repository uses [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The detailed [discovery attribution and IP policy](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) is currently a founding draft. Patent-sensitive disclosures and substantive contributions warrant appropriate review of those terms.

## Status

**Founding phase.** No AI system has been certified by Multa Lumina as passing either test. Both evaluation protocols are initial proposals open to research scrutiny; neither mission nor finish lines are being changed.

**Low barrier to participation. High bar for discovery claims.**
