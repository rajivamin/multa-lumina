# Start Here: Your First Five Minutes at Multa Lumina

Welcome! You do **not** need an advanced degree, professional experience, or the ability to train an AI model to participate.

## What are we investigating?

We are trying to develop an AI system that passes **either** of two complete tests:

- **The Full Job Test:** Independently perform an economically valuable full-time remote role, including unfamiliar onboarding, collaboration, policy changes, and weeks or months of real work.
- **The Wozniak Coffee Test:** Enter an unfamiliar American home, find the kitchen and the available supplies, and prepare coffee unassisted and safely.

An impressive partial demonstration is not a pass. Carefully documented failures still advance the investigation.

## Choose your entry point

**I'm curious or nontechnical:** Ask questions, suggest realistic workplace or household situations, identify flaws in explanations, or help translate research into plain English.

**I'm a beginner or vibe coder:** Choose one small, well-defined investigation. Use your preferred AI coding assistant, understand and test the result, and report accurately what happened.

**I'm an experienced developer or researcher:** Develop experiments, evaluation methods, agent systems, reproducibility tools, and independent reviews.

**I have real-world job or household expertise:** Explain what competent performance requires and which unexpected situations a meaningful test ought to include.

## An example first contribution

An AI customer success employee discovers the refund policy has changed from 30 days to 14 days, but an older company document still says 30 days.

Describe what an effective employee should do, what can go wrong, and what evidence would prove the AI handled the conflict correctly. No code is required to propose this scenario.

## How to participate

1. Read the [mission](README.md) and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. Check the repository's Issues and, once enabled, Discussions for related work.
3. Use a research-idea issue to propose a question or reproducible experiment.
4. Link earlier work, disclose relevant AI assistance, and never invent results.
5. Respect privacy, authorization, and safe testing boundaries.

## Five terms worth knowing

- **Agent:** An AI-driven system capable of using tools to pursue a task.
- **Autonomy:** Performing work without a person steering each step.
- **Benchmark:** A repeatable test with defined conditions.
- **Reproducibility:** Other people can independently check what happened.
- **Validation:** A specific claim has been reviewed against evidence; it does **not** necessarily mean either complete test has been passed.

Substantial code and patent-sensitive research need extra care. The repository carries an [Apache 2.0 license](LICENSE), but the detailed [research credit and IP policy](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) remains a founding draft. Read it before contributing.

**Our principle: low barrier to participation, high bar for claiming a discovery.**
