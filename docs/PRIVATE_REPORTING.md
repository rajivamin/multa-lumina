# Private Reporting & Research-Integrity Review

**Status: Protocol published; private intake address has not yet been activated.**

This procedure describes how Multa Lumina will handle sensitive concerns about contribution credit, research integrity, harassment, impersonation, or inappropriate publication. The reporting route must be tested and announced before contributors are instructed to submit reports.

## Reporting channels (keep separate)

- **Ordinary technical questions and public research critique:** Use [Discussions](https://github.com/rajivamin/multa-lumina/discussions) or Issues. Scientific disagreements based on publicly shareable evidence can be discussed openly without personal allegations.
- **Attribution disputes, plagiarism allegations, harassment, retaliation, or private evidence:** Use the dedicated private reporting mailbox **after it is published and verified**. **No private Multa Lumina mailbox has been designated yet. Do not disclose sensitive information in public Issues, Discussions, or pull requests.**
- **GitHub-hosted harassment or abuse:** GitHub's [Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) / GitHub Support tools may apply independently of the initiative.
- **Actual software security vulnerabilities:** Use a dedicated security-disclosure process, not the research-integrity mailbox by default. GitHub's Private Vulnerability Reporting is intended for security issues, not general authorship disputes.

## What a private report should contain, once intake is activated

1. A concise statement of the concern and what outcome is requested (correction, investigation, protection from harassment, etc.).
2. Specific links to the affected research records, Issues, Discussions, pull requests, or publications.
3. Available dated evidence and a clear explanation of the claimant's role or contribution.
4. Names or handles of people involved **only where necessary**.
5. Any immediate safety concern or reason not to contact a particular party.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential employer documents, unnecessary personal information, or other sensitive material. Ask the maintainers about a suitable secure transfer method before sending high-risk evidence.

## Triage and fairness

1. **Acknowledge and restrict access.** Once a functioning mailbox is in place, acknowledge reports promptly and limit access to authorized reviewers. Do not claim end-to-end encryption or anonymity unless the deployed channel actually provides it.
2. **Screen conflicts of interest.** The person whose conduct or credit is disputed cannot be the sole investigator or sole decision-maker. Seek an impartial outside reviewer when the maintainers are involved.
3. **Preserve evidence.** Retain links, dates, original statements and relevant version history. Keep private evidence outside public GitHub issues; minimize access and retention.
4. **Hear affected parties.** Give involved contributors a fair opportunity to explain or correct records, without exposing unrelated private information.
5. **Publish proportionate results.** Where justified, append a linked, evidence-based correction to the [Discovery Ledger](../DISCOVERY_LEDGER.md) and relevant records. Avoid gratuitous disclosure of private information or unsupported accusations.
6. **Allow appeal.** Provide a route for a different, impartial reviewer to reconsider a substantiated process error.
7. **Retain only what's needed.** Establish an explicit retention schedule and restricted-access archive before intake is launched. Do not promise absolute confidentiality or legal privilege.

## Limits

This initiative can correct *its own* attribution records, moderate contributions, and cite supporting evidence. It cannot conclusively determine legal inventorship, stop claims made on outside platforms, or replace an intellectual-property attorney. GitHub timestamps and hashes are evidence of an existing record, not proof of independent invention.

**Do not report privately through a GitHub issue. Issues and Discussions are public on this repository.**
