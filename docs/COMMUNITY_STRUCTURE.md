# Multa Lumina: Community Organization

This document describes the intended GitHub Discussions categories and the relationship between discussions, actionable Issues, research records, and discovery credit.

## Configure these Discussions categories

In the repository's **Discussions** tab, use the pencil next to Categories (or the category management control) to create or rename the following. Category titles are names for conversations, **not** alternative finish lines.

| Category | Format | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcements | Announcement | Verified initiative updates, policy changes, and plain-English research digests. Keep moderator-posted. |
| Welcome & Questions | Q&A | Beginner questions, how to get involved, terminology, and support. |
| Ideas & Hypotheses | Open-ended discussion | New theories or testable proposals relevant to either challenge, including non-code ideas. |
| Full Job Research | Open-ended discussion | Long-term job autonomy: onboarding, policy changes, workplace collaboration, economic value, and evaluation. |
| Coffee Test Research | Open-ended discussion | Unfamiliar home navigation, manipulation, robotics safety, and coffee preparation. |
| Findings & Replications | Open-ended discussion | Links to experiment records, replications, negative results, reviews, and limitations. No unverified claim is automatically a pass. |

Six categories are enough to begin. Use GitHub's native **General** category as Ideas & Hypotheses if renaming is easier, and retain any default categories you find helpful. Avoid proliferating narrowly differentiated categories.

## Where does something belong?

- **New person:** Start in Welcome & Questions.
- **Rough proposal:** Ideas & Hypotheses.
- **Substantive track debate:** Full Job Research or Coffee Test Research.
- **Actionable contribution with acceptance criteria:** An **Issue**, linking the discussion that sparked it.
- **Actual experiment with supporting evidence:** Use [research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md](../research/RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) in a pull request and link it from Findings & Replications.
- **Disputed attribution:** Use the private reporting route once published; do not post allegations or sensitive data in public discussions.

## Practical moderator guidance

Welcome contributors without testing credentials. Ask for evidence without attacking the person. Keep questions open; link duplicative ideas and earlier work. Encourage modest, testable studies. Separate *promising* results from *independently verified* results. Do not allow a flashy demo to quietly replace either official test.
