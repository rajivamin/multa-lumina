# How to Cite Multa Lumina Work

**Please credit the people responsible for the specific work, not only the repository owner.**

For an individual finding, cite its [Discovery Ledger](DISCOVERY_LEDGER.md) entry and the original contributor's research record or publication. Include the **named contributors and their roles**, title, version/date, permanent repository URL or DOI if available, and the evidence source.

Suggested template (replace all bracketed fields with verified information):

> [Contributors]. “[Exact research finding title].” Multa Lumina research record [ML-R-XXXXXX], [version and year]. [Permanent URL or DOI].

If you are citing the initiative's documentation rather than a specific discovery, identify it as the **Multa Lumina community repository** at https://github.com/rajivamin/multa-lumina and include the accessed revision or date.

No verified discovery records or research DOIs have yet been issued. No single contributor should be assigned authorship of all future work by default. For detailed expectations see [CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md](CREDIT_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).
